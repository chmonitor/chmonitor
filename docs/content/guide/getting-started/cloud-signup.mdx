chmonitor **Cloud** (`dash.chmonitor.dev`) is the hosted dashboard — sign in, connect ClickHouse or Postgres, and monitor. No infrastructure to run. Hosted Cloud does **not** sell seat or host plans.

<Callout type="info" title="Already self-hosting ClickHouse?">
Prefer [self-host](/operate/deploy/self-host) plus an optional [commercial license](/operate/advanced/commercial-license) (yearly or lifetime, honor system). That is the paid path for operators who already run infra.
</Callout>

## The sign-up flow

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in

Before signing in you can explore a live, public **read-only demo** cluster. Signing in hides the demo and shows your own setup.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect a host

There is no Polar plan picker. Add a ClickHouse or Postgres connection (see [ClickHouse user & grants](/guide/getting-started/clickhouse-requirements), or [Connect a firewalled ClickHouse](/guide/guides/connect-firewalled-clickhouse)).

Hosted Cloud does not cap hosts or seats. AI daily / monthly budget still apply on the hosted product.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Licenses (self-host)

Buy Team or Unlimited from [pricing](https://chmonitor.dev/pricing/) or Cloud **/billing**. Polar checkout is for the **self-host license**, then [register](https://chmonitor.dev/license/register) your company. See [commercial license](/operate/advanced/commercial-license).
